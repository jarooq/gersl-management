## 🔄 **Workflow Integration**

### Complete CBO Partnership Lifecycle:

```
Step 1: CBO Registration
   ↓
Step 2: Due Diligence Assessment
   ↓ (If Approved)
Step 3: CBO Submits Proposal
   ↓
Step 4: Proposal Review & Approval
   ↓ (If Approved)
Step 5: Project Implementation
   ↓
Step 6: Progress Monitoring & Reporting
   ↓
Step 7: Project Completion
```

### Decision Points:
1. **Due Diligence < 70**: Capacity building required before proposal submission
2. **Due Diligence 70-84**: Approved for small-scale projects
3. **Due Diligence ≥85**: Approved for complex, high-value projects
4. **Proposal Score < 70**: Rejected (can resubmit after revisions)
5. **Proposal Score ≥70**: Approved for implementation
